Shift & Earnings Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Your shift data stays on your phone. Shifts, pay rates and car details are stored only on your device and in your own Android/Google backup. We have no servers and never receive this data.

Location (optional). If you turn on "Track miles with GPS", the app uses your phone's precise location only while the shift timer is running, including when the app is in the background during that shift, to measure how far you drive. A notification is shown for the whole time it's on, and it stops when you finish the shift. The route is saved only on your phone and is used to fill in your distance, split it per delivery, show driving vs waiting time and draw your earnings map, all on the device. It is never uploaded, shared or sold, and deleting a shift deletes its route. Tapping "Map" next to a waiting spot passes that one point to your own maps app. You can turn tracking off at any time in Settings or in Android's app permissions.

Google Drive backup (optional). If you connect your Google account, a backup file is saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can open. We can't see it. You can turn it off and delete it in Settings.

Notifications (optional). Only used for the vehicle reminders you set (MOT, road tax, insurance, service). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Company logos. Logos for the companies you add are downloaded from Google's public favicon service (only the company's web address is sent) and cached on your phone.

In-app review. After a few shifts the app may show Google Play's rating card. Google handles it; we receive no personal data.

Ads. The app shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases. The app is currently free with no in-app purchases.

Children. This app is not directed at children under 13.
